Production steps

1. Prototype the models and test with 10 classes.
2. Have two embryologists review the content.
3. Mould the high-volume parts and 3D-print the rest.
4. Pilot in schools and an anatomy department, then revise.
5. Sell one shared core, a low-cost school edition, and a university edition with add-on packs.
